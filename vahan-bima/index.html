How a quote actually reaches you

This is the exact path your request takes — nothing happens behind the scenes.

  1. 1
    You fill the form

    Vehicle type, policy type, registration number, mobile, email and city.

  2. 2
    It reaches us on WhatsApp

    Your details land directly on our WhatsApp Business number — no waiting room.

  3. 3
    We check live quotes

    We pull real quotes for your vehicle from the PolicyBazaar portal.

  4. 4
    You get the policy details

    Coverage, premium and insurer name — shared with you on WhatsApp.

  5. 5
    A payment scanner is shared

    A UPI scanner from the insurer's own portal (valid ~15 minutes).

  6. 6
    It's sent straight to you

    Screenshot shared on WhatsApp the moment it's generated.

  7. 7
    You pay the insurer directly

    Straight to the payment gateway. We never see or hold your money.

  8. 8
    Your policy is issued

    Digitally, by the insurer, in your name — usually within minutes.
